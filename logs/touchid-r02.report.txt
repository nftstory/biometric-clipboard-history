Root cause
- popupDidClose() deactivates the biometric gate and refreshes History.items from the full history.
- prepareForPopupOpen() restored the gated set before orderFrontRegardless(), but ContentView applies an implicit animation keyed to history.items.
- The hidden NSHostingView could therefore retain the full-list backing state and paint it while SwiftUI animated the full-to-gated change.

Fix chosen
- AppState.prepareForPopupOpen() now activates and refreshes the gate inside a Transaction with animations disabled.
- FloatingPanel.open() forces the still-hidden hosting view to lay out and display that state before orderFrontRegardless().
- The post-Touch-ID biometricGateDidUnlock() path is unchanged, so its in-popup expansion may still animate.
- History filtering, search, command-number shortcuts, and navigation logic are unchanged.
- Grace-window opens still refresh directly to the full source without a gated transition.
