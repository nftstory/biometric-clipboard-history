Root cause
- popupDidClose() restores the full history in the hidden panel, so Popup.height retained the full-history measurement.
- prepareForPopupOpen() switched History.items to the gated set, but FloatingPanel.open() sized the panel from the stale supplied height before forcing the hidden SwiftUI layout.
- Round 02 therefore fixed the first painted content but pre-drew it inside the already selected full stored height; the flexible ScrollView became the black gap and left the separately measured unlock row at the bottom.
- The unlock row was already included correctly in extraBottomHeight; its placement was not the source of the gap.

Fix chosen
- HistoryListView now continuously retains the measured scroll-content height in Popup.historyListHeight.
- FloatingPanel.open() activates the gate, lays out the still-hidden hosting view, refreshes Popup.height from the gated list plus header/top/bottom/footer measurements, and only then sizes, pre-draws, and orders the panel front.
- Popup.preferredHeight() again uses stock Maccy's minimum-then-stored-maximum clamp ordering.
- The existing biometricGateDidUnlock() needsResize path is unchanged, preserving the animated in-popup expansion and the stored-height cap.
- Search, command-number, arrow navigation, grace-window, and unlock-row layout logic are unchanged.
